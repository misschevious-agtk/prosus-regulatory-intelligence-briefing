# When is a certification mark a certification mark?

**Source:** [IPKat blog](http://ipkitten.blogspot.com/2026/09/when-is-certification-mark.html)
**Published:** 2026-09-26T11:41:47.647+01:00
**Matched keywords (2):** Patent, patent

## Summary excerpt

Certification marks are a rare breed. But rare does not mean uncontroversial. Most issues surrounding them have not reached the courts (yet). One of these issues is whether a certification mark must indicate to consumers that it is a certification mark and not an individual mark. This has been the subject of a recent decision from the German Patent Court (“GPC”, case 26 W (pat) 579/20).
