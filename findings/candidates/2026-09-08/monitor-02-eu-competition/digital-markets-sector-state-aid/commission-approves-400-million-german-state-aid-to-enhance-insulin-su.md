# Commission approves €400 million German State aid to enhance insulin supply resilience

**Source:** [European Commission press](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1798)
**Published:** Mon, 07 Sep 2026 22:00:00 GMT
**Matched keywords (5):** European Commission, resilience, state aid, service of general economic interest, SGEI

## Summary excerpt

European Commission Press release Brussels, 08 Sep 2026 The European Commission has approved, under EU State aid rules, a €400 million German measure in favour of Sanofi-Aventis Deutschland GmbH ('Sanofi') to strengthen the resilience of the supply of human insulin and insulin analogues ('insulins'). Under the measure, Germany will pay Sanofi a public service compensation to provide a service of general economic interest ('SGEI').
