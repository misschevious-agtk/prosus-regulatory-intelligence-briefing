## Summary excerpt

Does a cartel infringement decision nullify contracts concluded downstream of the cartel between non-cartel participants? In Livronsa (C-60/25), the Court of Justice of the European Union (“CJEU”) gave this question a firm answer against the backdrop of the Euribor manipulation scandal. By limiting both the automatic nullity under Article 101(2) TFEU and the binding effect of Commission infringement decisions, the judgment raises important questions about the consequences of anticompetitive conduct across interconnected markets and the scope of private enforcement.
