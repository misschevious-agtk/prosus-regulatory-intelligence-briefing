## Summary excerpt

In July, the European Commission issued a press release communicating that it had fined Google for preventing app developers from freely communicating and promoting offers and concluding contracts with users in distribution channels of their choice, including third-party app stores. Two months later, in September, the DMA’s enforcer published the non-compliance decision relating to how Google had breached the steering mandate under Article 5(4) DMA. The case jumped on the bandwagon of the European Commission’s previous non-compliance decision against Apple for similar reasons (see comment here ).
