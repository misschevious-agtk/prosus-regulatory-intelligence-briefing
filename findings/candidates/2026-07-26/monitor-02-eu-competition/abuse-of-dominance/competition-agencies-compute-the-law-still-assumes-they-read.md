## Summary excerpt

All 27 EU national competition agencies, DG Competition, and most agencies worldwide now rely on computational tools to enforce competition law. Spain screens public procurement for bid rigging with BRAVA, a supervised machine learning system that maps relationships between firms, bids, and individuals. Brazil runs procurement documents through Cerebro to surface signs of collusion. France queries its case database in natural language through a retrieval augmented generation system built on large language models.
