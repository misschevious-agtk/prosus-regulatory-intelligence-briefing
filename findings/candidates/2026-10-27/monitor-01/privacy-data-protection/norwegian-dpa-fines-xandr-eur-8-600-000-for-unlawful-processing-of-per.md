Background information Date of final decision: 2 October 2026 National case&nbsp; Controller: Xandr Inc. Legal References: Article 6 (Lawfulness of processing), Article 9 (Processing of special categories of personal data) Decision: Administrative fine Key words: Fines, Basic principles, Marketing, Online platforms Summary of the Decision Origin of the case &nbsp; The case started in 2020 when the Norwegian Consumer Council lodged a complaint against the advertising technology company Xandr (at the time AppNexus, now a part of Microsoft) with the Norwegian DPA (Data Protection Authority). The case concerns real-time bidding in Grindr, a location-based dating app aimed at gay, bi, trans and queer people. Key Findings&nbsp; The Norwegian DPA has found it proven by clear preponderance of probability that Xandr processed Grindr users’ personal data, at least the fact that individuals were using Grindr, their advertising ID and IP addresses, during real-time bidding. These are personal data under Article 4(1) GDPR and data concerning sexual orientation under Article 9(1) GDPR.
