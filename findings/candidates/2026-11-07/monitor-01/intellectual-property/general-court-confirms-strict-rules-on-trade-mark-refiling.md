# General Court confirms strict rules on trade mark refiling

**Source:** [IPKat blog](http://ipkitten.blogspot.com/2026/11/general-court-confirms-strict-rules-on.html)
**Published:** 2026-11-05T06:11:07.140+00:00
**Matched keywords (4):** genuine use, EUIPO, bad faith, trade mark

## Summary excerpt

Establishing genuine use of a trade mark can be a burdensome task. What would be easier than refiling the trade mark a few months before or after the five-year grace period for non-use expires? As easy as it appears, this approach might cost the refiled trade mark because of bad faith, as the General Court held in Qingdao Ecopure Filter Co. Ltd v EUIPO (Waterdrop) (case T-541/25).
