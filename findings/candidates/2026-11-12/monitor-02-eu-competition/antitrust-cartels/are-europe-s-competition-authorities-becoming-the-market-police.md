# Are Europe’s Competition Authorities Becoming the Market Police?

**Published:** 2026-11-11T09:00:00.000Z

## Summary excerpt

Unannounced inspections, searches of private homes, sealed offices, forensic copies of entire mailboxes, police officers at the door – and, if a current Polish bill is enacted, an official who buys a subscription under an assumed name to see whether it can be cancelled. EU enforcement law has steadily equipped administrative authorities with methods long associated with criminal investigations. Are national competition authorities (NCAs) becoming a form of market police? The driving force is competition law. The ECN+ Directive 2019/1 completed what Regulation 1/2003 had begun: it obliged Member States to give every NCA a common core of coercive powers modelled on those of the Commission.
