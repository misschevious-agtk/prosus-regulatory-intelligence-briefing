# THE SUPREME COURT SETS THE MINIMUM MARK-UP AT 5 PER CENT

**Source:** [Kluwer Competition Law Blog](https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/competition-blog/the-supreme-court-sets-the-minimum-mark-up-at-5-per-cent/)
**Published:** 2026-11-02T09:00:00.000Z
**Matched keywords (1):** CNMC

## Summary excerpt

The Supreme Court has just issued two judgements that appear to lay the foundations for the future of litigation in the so-called “car cartel” case. The car cartel involved the exchange of confidential information between manufacturers and distributors in Spain, mainly between 2006 and 2013 1 . The manufacturers shared data on profit margins, discounts and commercial strategies, thereby reducing competition between brands and causing harm to consumers. The Spanish Competition Authority (“ CNMC ”) imposed fines totaling 171 million euros on the cartel members in 2015, and the Supreme Court upheld the fines against the car manufacturers in 2021 (except for the Volkswagen Group, which joined the leniency programme, and Mazda).
