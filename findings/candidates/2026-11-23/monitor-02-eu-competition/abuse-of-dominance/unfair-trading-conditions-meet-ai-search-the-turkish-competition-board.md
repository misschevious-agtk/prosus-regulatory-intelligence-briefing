In April 2026, Todd Davies argued on this blog that letting publishers opt out of AI Overviews would not cure the competitive harm those summaries cause, and that interim measures under Article 102 TFEU might be needed before a final decision arrives too late. Six months later, the first interim measure of that kind came not from Brussels but from Ankara. On 2 November 2026, the Turkish Competition Authority announced that the Competition Board had opened an investigation into Google's "AI Overviews" and "AI Mode" features and, by a decision of 8 October 2026, had imposed interim measures under Article 9(4) of Law No 4054 on the Protection of Competition.
